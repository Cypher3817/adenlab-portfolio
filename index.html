The 2020/2021 Origin

This project began as my final-year university computer science capstone in 2020/2021. While it earned a passing grade for its architectural design and core concepts, time constraints meant the full end-to-end vision was never completely finished.

The 2026 Ground-Up Rebuild

Rather than leaving it as a static archive, I am rebuilding the entire platform from scratch using modern tooling. It functions simultaneously as a production smart-home automation engine and a public portfolio sandbox powered by realistic dummy telemetry.
